# Shared visual style

Every image prompt starts with this block, word for word, so all parts of all
slides look like one series:

```
Cute cartoon visual mnemonic for medical students, children's educational poster style: bright flat colors, thick dark outlines, pure white background with nothing touching the image edges unless it is meant to continue into the next part. Friendly characters with big eyes and smiles. All labels in rounded white boxes with dark navy bold text and a thin pointer line to the thing they name; text crisp and spelled exactly as given.
```

Recurring characters (reuse them whenever the topic appears, so students meet old friends):

| Concept | Character |
|---|---|
| Glucose | yellow hexagon with a smiling face (a crowned king when it is the hero) |
| Other monosaccharides | hexagons/pentagons in other colors (galactose pink, fructose green) |
| Glycogen | a box/crate of stacked yellow hexagons |
| Liver | smiling brown liver with white cartoon gloves |
| Blood | red vessel with red cells flowing left to right |
| Disease / defect | a cracked robot or a warning sign |
| Enzyme | a small worker with a tool, the enzyme name on its cap |
| Aldehyde (CHO) | top hat on the head; ketone (C=O) is a belt buckle at the waist |
