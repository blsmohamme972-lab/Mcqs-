Picmonic-style visual mnemonic, fun and medically accurate, landscape poster for medical students: cute cartoon characters with big friendly eyes, bright flat colors, thick dark outlines, clean pale background, each medical fact drawn as a memorable visual pun. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers or labels anywhere in the image: pictures only.
The story reads left to right.
LEFT: a smiling cartoon woman's face seen from the front. A glowing red triangle covers the area from the corners of the mouth up to the bridge of the nose, marked with a yellow warning sign. Inside the triangle a grumpy pimple-boil crawls with a yellow cluster of grapes (bacteria).
MIDDLE: a winding blue vein shaped like an open water slide with no gates or valves, carrying the yellow grape bacteria up toward the eye socket and down into a big dark cave in the skull.
RIGHT: the cave entrance with a plain wooden signboard (blank). Inside the cave a purple jelly blood clot plugs the space. Five colored cables run through the cave, and a thick red pipe (the carotid artery) pulses like a drum with pulse lines.
TOP RIGHT: the woman's eye bulges out of the socket on a spring (proptosis) with a swollen puffy jelly ring of fluid around it (chemosis) and heartbeat pulse lines. A lightning bolt shows eye pain and a hot red thermometer shows fever.
