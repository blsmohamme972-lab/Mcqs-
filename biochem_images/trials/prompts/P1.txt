Picmonic-style visual mnemonic, fun and medically accurate, landscape poster for medical students: cute cartoon characters with big friendly eyes, bright flat colors, thick dark outlines, clean pale background, each medical fact drawn as a memorable visual pun. All text sits in rounded white label boxes with dark navy bold letters, crisp and spelled exactly as given, very few words.
The story reads left to right.
LEFT: a smiling cartoon woman's face seen from the front. A glowing red triangle covers the area from the corners of the mouth up to the bridge of the nose, marked with a yellow warning sign. Inside the triangle a grumpy pimple-boil crawls with a yellow cluster of grapes (bacteria). Label: "Infected facial lesion".
MIDDLE: a winding blue vein shaped like an open water slide with no gates or valves, carrying the yellow grape bacteria up toward the eye socket and down into a big dark cave in the skull. Label: "Valveless veins".
RIGHT: the cave entrance with a signboard: "CAVERNOUS SINUS". Inside the cave a purple jelly blood clot plugs the space. Five colored cables run through the cave, labeled "CN III IV V1 V2 VI", and a thick red pipe (the carotid artery) pulses like a drum with pulse lines, labeled "Carotid artery".
TOP RIGHT: the woman's eye bulges out of the socket on a spring (proptosis) with a swollen puffy jelly ring of fluid around it (chemosis) and heartbeat pulse lines. Labels: "Pulsating proptosis" and "Chemosis". A lightning bolt labeled "Ocular pain" and a hot thermometer labeled "Fever".
