Picmonic-style visual mnemonic and teaching poster, fun and medically accurate, landscape, for medical students: cute cartoon characters with big friendly eyes, bright flat colors, thick dark outlines, clean pale background, each medical fact drawn as a memorable visual pun. All text sits in rounded white label boxes with dark navy bold letters, crisp and spelled exactly as given.
Title banner at the top: "Cavernous Sinus Thrombosis".
LEFT: a smiling cartoon woman's face seen from the front. A glowing red triangle covers the area from the corners of the mouth up to the bridge of the nose, marked with a yellow warning sign. Inside the triangle a grumpy pimple-boil crawls with a yellow cluster of grapes (bacteria). Labels: "Danger triangle of the face", "Infected lesion", "Staph aureus".
MIDDLE: a winding blue vein shaped like an open water slide with no gates, carrying the grape bacteria up toward the eye socket and down into a big dark cave in the skull. Labels: "Angular vein", "Ophthalmic vein", "No valves".
RIGHT: the cave entrance with a signboard: "CAVERNOUS SINUS". Inside the cave a purple jelly blood clot plugs the space ("Thrombosis"). Five colored cables run through it, labeled "CN III", "CN IV", "CN V1", "CN V2", "CN VI", and a thick red pipe pulses like a drum, labeled "Internal carotid artery".
TOP RIGHT: the woman's eye bulges out on a spring with a puffy jelly ring around it and heartbeat pulse lines. Labels: "Pulsating proptosis", "Chemosis", "Ocular pain", "Fever".
BOTTOM banner: "Answer: Cavernous sinus".
