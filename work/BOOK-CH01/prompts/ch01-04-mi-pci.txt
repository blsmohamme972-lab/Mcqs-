TOPIC: When was the last heart attack?  (book: Cardiothoracic Surgery 2e, chapter: Preoperative assessment, pages Ch 1 · 2–3).
Teach exactly these facts, no others; every label text must come from them:
- When was the last heart attack?
- For every previous infarction, document the number, the dates, and the coronary anatomy.
- Check the chart, and look for Q waves on the EKG.
- Surgical risk is higher after a recent STEMI than after an NSTEMI.
- And it is highest early after the infarct, falling after one week.
- Now previous stents and angioplasty. Note the left ventricular function and coronary anatomy from the angiography report.
- Record how many procedures, when, what type, and the result.
- Emergency surgery after a failed PCI is high risk.
- And after a recent PCI, clopidogrel usually continues for at least one month, which can change how bypass is done.
- Count, date, anatomy, timing. Then the stent story.
