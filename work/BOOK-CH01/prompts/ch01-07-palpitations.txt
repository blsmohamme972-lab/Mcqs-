TOPIC: What does a palpitation feel like?  (book: Cardiothoracic Surgery 2e, chapter: Preoperative assessment, pages 3).
Teach exactly these facts, no others; every label text must come from them:
- What does a palpitation actually feel like?
- To the patient it may be skipped beats, a racing heart, an irregular rhythm, or simply awareness of the heartbeat, especially at night.
- Then ask about onset and offset.
- Rapid onset and offset suggests a paroxysmal arrhythmia: it switches on and off.
- Gradual onset and offset suggests sinus tachycardia: it fades in and out.
- Preoperative atrial fibrillation may need ablation, left atrial appendage ligation, and warfarin.
- Now swelling. Peripheral edema can follow changes in diuretic therapy, weight gain, or worse breathlessness.
- It helps quantify the heart failure.
- Ask what it felt like, how it started and stopped, and whether the ankles swell.
