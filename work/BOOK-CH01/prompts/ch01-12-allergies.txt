TOPIC: Allergies that change the operation  (book: Cardiothoracic Surgery 2e, chapter: Preoperative assessment, pages 4).
Teach exactly these facts, no others; every label text must come from them:
- Allergies: the ones that change the operation.
- Take every drug, dose and regimen, and every allergy: drugs, iodine, contrast, and fish.
- Fish matters because protamine is made from salmon sperm.
- On bypass, the blood is anticoagulated with heparin, and protamine reverses it at the end.
- Latex allergy means changing the equipment, so warn the operating room.
- Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia is the opposite problem: heparin itself is the culprit.
- Those patients may need a heparin-free bypass with bivalirudin or argatroban, which mandates careful pre-planning.
- Iodine and contrast allergies matter for every scan and angiogram that follows.
- Fish, latex, heparin, contrast: four allergies, four plans.
