TOPIC: Arteries elsewhere change the operation  (book: Cardiothoracic Surgery 2e, chapter: Preoperative assessment, pages 3).
Teach exactly these facts, no others; every label text must come from them:
- Arteries elsewhere change the operation here.
- Ask specifically about TIAs, strokes, claudication, and vascular interventions.
- A history of TIAs warrants a carotid duplex to exclude carotid disease.
- Because arteriopathy outside the heart feeds the risk score, changes the stroke and limb-ischemia risk you quote at consent, and influences the conduit and cannulation site.
- Then the risk factors. Some can be fixed in hospital: smoking, cholesterol, obesity, hypertension and diabetes.
- Some cannot: male sex, age, and family history.
- Be precise. Non-smoker is not the same as smoked 60 a day and stopped two weeks ago.
- Check the carotids. Fix what can be fixed. Be exact.
